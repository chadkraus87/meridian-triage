Meridian Cloud has three plans: Free, Pro, and Enterprise. You manage your plan and billing details in the dashboard.

## Find your invoices

Invoices are in Settings > Billing. You can view and download each invoice as a PDF. Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month.


## Change your billing details

Go to Settings > Billing to change any of these:

- **Plan:** upgrade or downgrade between Free, Pro, and Enterprise. For how changes are charged, see KB-020.
- **Billing email:** the address that receives invoices and payment notices. This address is also used to confirm the account owner in lockout cases (see KB-005).
- **VAT ID:** add or update your VAT number so it appears on future invoices.
- **Card:** update the credit or debit card used for payment.

Changes to the VAT ID or billing email apply to future invoices. Past invoices are not reissued automatically.

## Plan limits at a glance

| | Free | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Users | 3 | 50 | custom |
| API rate limit per key | 60/min | 600/min | 3,000/min |

See KB-021 for seats and KB-009 for API rate limits.

## Questions about a charge

If a charge looks wrong, contact support with:

- Your account ID (from Settings > Account)
- The invoice number and date
- What you expected to be charged

Never send full card numbers in a ticket. The last 4 digits are enough.

For refunds and failed payments, see KB-020.
