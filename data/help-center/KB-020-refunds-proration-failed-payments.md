This article explains how plan changes are charged, how refund requests work, and what happens when a payment fails.

## Upgrades are prorated

When you upgrade (for example Free to Pro, or Pro to Enterprise), upgrades are prorated immediately. You are charged for the remaining part of the current billing cycle at the new price, and the new plan's limits apply right away.

## Downgrades take effect next cycle

When you downgrade, the change takes effect next cycle. You keep the current plan until the end of the billing cycle. There are no partial refunds for the unused part of the cycle.

Before downgrading, check you are within the lower plan's limits, such as seats (see KB-021).

## Refund requests

Refund requests are reviewed by the billing team. Support can't promise or issue a refund directly. To request one, contact support with:

- Your account ID (from Settings > Account)
- The invoice number and date
- The amount and the reason for the request

The billing team will reply with a decision.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails (for example the card is declined or expired):

1. Meridian emails the billing email address.
2. Failed payments retry 3 times over 7 days.
3. If all retries fail, the workspace becomes read-only. Your data is kept, but you can't make changes until payment succeeds.

To fix it, go to Settings > Billing, update the card, and retry the payment. The workspace returns to normal once payment succeeds.

Never send full card numbers in a ticket. The last 4 digits are enough.
